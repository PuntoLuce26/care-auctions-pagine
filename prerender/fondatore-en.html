Gianluca Genco — founder of CAReAuctions™

Gianluca Genco founded CAReAuctions™, the Italian platform dedicated to judicial real-estate auctions, and created iCARe, the adviser that answers in the site's chat. CAReAuctions™ acts as a buyer-side adviser: it does not broker deals, takes no commission, and charges a success fee only when the auction is awarded. The company behind the platform is PUNTO LUCE S.R.L.S., based in Cagliari, Italy.
