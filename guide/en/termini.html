Auction deadlines: dates, terms and what happens when they change

Auctions are also won by respecting dates. Deadlines are not paperwork: one hour late on a filing can cost you the lot.

1 · Publication of the notice

The sale notice is published on the PVP portal and, where required, in newspapers. It states date and time of the auction, the method (synchronous, online or without bidding), the starting price, the minimum bid and the bid deadline. Every term counts from here.

Source: PVP — sale notice of the procedure

2 · The bid deadline

Bids are filed by the deadline in the notice, normally at least one day before the auction (the judge may set it differently). Online filing closes at the stated hour: there is no grace period.

Source: Italian Ministry of Justice — sales and auctions

3 · The auction date

At the stated time the judge (or delegate) opens the envelopes, checks formalities and opens the bidding. Online auctions run for a window defined by the notice, with bids raised on the portal.

Source: Online sale rules (art. 574-bis, Code of Civil Procedure)

4 · Postponement, suspension, applications

An auction can be postponed or suspended (debtor application, publication error, appeal). Bidders learn about it from the portal or the registry — which is why notice and lot page must be re-read until the day before.

Source: PVP — status of the procedure

5 · Deadlines after the award

Once awarded, the balance must be paid within the term set by the judge (often 60-120 days). Missing it means forfeiture and loss of the deposit as the order provides.

Source: Award order (documents of the procedure)

The official sources

Everything here can be checked on public sources: the Italian Ministry of Justice Public Sales Portal publishes notices, valuation reports and outcomes of every procedure; giustizia.it explains sales and auctions; the law is on Normattiva.
