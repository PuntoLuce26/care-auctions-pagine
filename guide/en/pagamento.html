Paying for the auction: balance, taxes and costs (and what happens if you don't)

The auction price is not only your bid. Balance, taxes and costs can add several thousand euros: calculate them before bidding, not after.

1 · The balance

The balance is paid within the term set in the award order (often 60-120 days), by the means the order and the delegate prescribe.

Source: Award order — term and method of payment

2 · Purchase taxes

Auction purchases pay registration and mortgage taxes as any sale: normally 2% of the price for a first home (where the legal requirements are met) and 9% otherwise, plus fixed duties and VAT where due. Rates must be checked on the actual case with a professional.

Source: Italian Revenue Agency — taxes on property purchases

3 · Procedure costs

On top of taxes: procedure costs (custody, advertising, delegate and expert fees) and condominium arrears, which follow specific rules in judicial sales. Read them in the documents before paying.

Source: Sale notice and valuation report — charges and costs

4 · If the balance does not arrive

Missing the term means forfeiture of the award and loss of the deposit, plus a new sale attempt. It is the most expensive and most avoidable risk: calculate it before bidding.

Source: Court order — forfeiture and new attempt

5 · The full sum, before bidding

Price + taxes + procedure costs + condominium arrears + works + fees: that is the turnkey cost. Only that figure is compared with the OMI market value, never the starting price alone.

Source: Turnkey Price — the real cost (CAReAuctions)

The official sources

Everything here can be checked on public sources: the Italian Ministry of Justice Public Sales Portal publishes notices, valuation reports and outcomes of every procedure; giustizia.it explains sales and auctions; the law is on Normattiva.
