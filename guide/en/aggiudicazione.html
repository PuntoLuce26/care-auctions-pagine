The award: how an auction is won and what happens next

Winning is not the end: it is when obligations become yours. Knowing how the award works prevents losing the deposit over a formality.

1 · Opening of the bids

At the stated time the judge opens the envelopes (or closes the online window), checks that each bid is formal, that the deposit is paid and that the price is not below the minimum bid. Irregular bids are excluded.

Source: PVP — auction record of the procedure

2 · The bidding

With more than one bid, bidding opens under the rules of the notice, with a stated minimum increment. In auctions without bidding the highest offer normally wins; with bidding, the price is raised in rounds.

Source: Italian Code of Civil Procedure — sale with and without bidding

3 · The award

The judge awards the lot (usually provisionally) to the best bidder and sets the term for the balance. From then on the buyer is bound: withdrawing means losing the deposit.

Source: Award order (documents of the procedure)

4 · Unsold auctions

If nobody bids, the judge orders a new attempt, usually at a price reduced by up to 25%. An unsold auction is not a closed one: the lot returns, often on better terms — and those who were following it know first.

Source: PVP — auction outcomes (unsold and next attempt)

5 · Checks before paying

Before the balance, check procedure costs, taxes, condominium arrears and the condition of the property. The full economic picture is built here, not after the transfer decree.

Source: Sale notice and valuation report — costs and charges

The official sources

Everything here can be checked on public sources: the Italian Ministry of Justice Public Sales Portal publishes notices, valuation reports and outcomes of every procedure; giustizia.it explains sales and auctions; the law is on Normattiva.
